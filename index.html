How Prop Score works

The score (1–99) ranks how mispriced a prop looks, not how often it hits. For both the over and the under, the model:

  • Blends the hit rate over the last 10, last 5, and season at that side's best line. Small samples are pulled toward what the market already says, so a hot streak alone can't fool it.
  • Nudges it for the opponent's defense against that position and stat.
  • Compares the result to the chance the price implies. The bigger the gap, the higher the score.

Props are hidden unless the player has played 7 of the last 10 weeks, has a real role (targets, carries, or attempts), the line isn't tiny, and the price isn't juiced worse than about −255. Tap Show under the list to see them.

It's a research shortcut, not a prediction. The books see most of this too, so check the chart and matchup before you bet.
